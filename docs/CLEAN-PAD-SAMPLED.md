# Static Pad / Recorded Instruments revision

User direction: clean pads without howling or conspicuous filter changes; prioritize realism with real samples.

All 175 factory presets categorized Pad now use low resonance and zero filter-envelope/LFO sweep. One pad modulation slots, ring/FM and distortion are disabled; SVF is low-pass. PolyBrute pad modulation slots, FM/metalizer/distortion are disabled. Filter morph targets and relevant patch cables / Continuum cutoff matrix routes are removed. This is a factory voicing change, not a global ban on resonance in the synthesizer controls. Saved projects retain their stored values; reselect factory presets to use revised patches.

All four plugins offer four recorded programs, tagged `[Sampled]`: Upright KW piano, violin section, cello section, solo violin. Previous Atelier piano and bowed-string factory studies, plus One's Hammer Body and Bow Section reworks, select their corresponding recorded program when freshly loaded. Other synthesized instrument studies are still approximations and have not been upgraded to samples.

Source selection is available through the sampled presets (INIT returns to synthesis); source and sample gain are also host automation parameters. They do not add fictitious controls to the hardware panels. Source selection and gain are appended host parameters; previous synthesis parameter indexes remain unchanged. Program zero retains the original synthesis engine. Recorded programs replace the synthesis audio, not double it; the hardware filter knobs do not process these recorded programs. Instrument master volume and global OUT still control their output, with short gain smoothing to avoid abrupt level steps. This is an added sample instrument feature, not an emulation of sample playback in the original hardware. MIDI velocity, release, sustain, two-semitone pitch bend and immediate all-sound-off are supported. The sample banks are loaded and voices/channels allocated during prepare, not on the audio callback. Switching sources clears old voices. Source/gain use the existing saved-state parameter mechanism.

Each distributable contains its own licensed sample assets, resolved relative to the module; development tools fall back to assets/samples. Windows standalone packages include adjacent EntroSamples. Sources, exact sample hashes, adaptation limits and licenses: [SOURCES.md](../assets/samples/SOURCES.md). The piano has two recorded velocity layers, not a many-layer concert grand. Strings retain finite sustained recordings and their original ranges; there are no sampled legato transitions, round robin or full articulation library. Musical realism still requires listening review.
