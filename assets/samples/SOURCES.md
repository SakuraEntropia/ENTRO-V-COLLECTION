# Recorded instrument sources

- `UprightKW.sf2`: unmodified Upright Piano KW 2022-02-21, recorded by Gonzalo and Roberto for FreePats. Kawai upright, stereo, two velocity layers. CC0 1.0. Original readme and full CC0 text included. https://freepats.zenvoid.org/Piano/acoustic-grand-piano.html
- `ViolinSection.sf2`, `CelloSection.sf2`, `SoloViolin.sf2`: adaptation of VSCO 2 Community Edition tag 1.1.0 by Versilian Studios / Sam Gossner. CC0 1.0. Maps and recordings from https://github.com/sgossner/VSCO-2-CE/tree/1.1.0 ; official project https://versilian-studios.com/vsco-community/ . Source WAV hashes in VSCO-source-checksums.json. Conversion script: scripts/build-vsco-sf2.py. Original key/velocity regions, fine tuning and relative gain retained; conversion to stereo 16-bit 44.1 kHz with a fixed -13.98 dB gain offset. Finite recordings, no artificial sustain loops, no legato transition samples, no round robin added. No claim of a complete orchestral performance library.
- Playback: TinySoundFont by Bernhard Schelling, based on SFZero by Steve Folta. MIT license included in third_party/tinysoundfont/LICENSE. The header is vendored; its exact digest is recorded in SHA256SUMS.

Sampled programs bypass the analog synthesis path. They are added recorded instruments, not original hardware sounds. Synthesis remains program zero. Source selection and sample gain are host parameters and survive state saving. Fixed playback bank gains (dB): piano -12, violin section +6, cello section -6, solo violin 0. These compensate the differing recording levels; no automatic compression, limiting or normalization is applied.
